Tips

Flights (Frontier)

  • ⚠️ Personal item only — no free carry-on or checked bag
  • Personal item = small backpack or purse that fits under the seat
  • Carry-on bag: ~$60–99 each way if you add it
  • Consider packing light in one personal item + wearing your bulkiest items on the plane
  • Check in online 24 hours before for free seat selection

Hotel

  • Check-in: 4:00 PM | Checkout: 11:00 AM
  • Must be 21+ with photo ID and credit card at check-in (cash not accepted)
  • Free cancellation until November 10, 2026
  • Outside alcohol limit: 1 bottle/person or 6 cans/person (max 2 bottles or 12 cans per room)
  • Go Pool is heated and 21+ — free for hotel guests

Weather (Mid-November)

  • Highs: ~65–68°F (pleasant during the day)
  • Lows: ~42–45°F (chilly at night!)
  • Rain: Very unlikely (5% chance)
  • Bring: Light jacket or blazer for evenings, layers

Money

  • Vegas is cashless-friendly but bring some cash for tips, valet, etc.
  • ATMs on the Strip charge $5–8 fees — withdraw before you go
  • Tipping: $1–2/drink at bars, 18–20% at restaurants, $2–5/day housekeeping

Getting Around

  • Flamingo is center-Strip — most things are walkable
  • Uber/Lyft for airport runs (~$15–25, surge during events)
  • The Deuce bus runs the full Strip for $6/2-hour pass or $8/24-hour
  • Monorail also connects Strip hotels ($5 single ride)

High Roller (Included)

  • Located at LINQ Promenade — literally attached to Flamingo's back exit
  • 30-minute rotation, 550 feet high
  • Best at sunset (~4:45 PM in November)
  • Open bar cabin upgrade available (~$50–70 if you want to splurge, but your drinks are already free at bars)
